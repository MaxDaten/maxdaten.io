## Platform

web

## Users

- **Primary:** technical decision-makers (CTO, VP Engineering, engineering lead) at startups and
  scale-ups, mostly in the DACH region. They arrive through a referral, LinkedIn or the blog and are
  deciding whether to bring in a hands-on freelancer. Their question: can this person fix our
  delivery, platform or product problem with us, and leave the team self-sufficient?
- **Secondary:** engineers reading the blog and the gems.
- **Undecided:** whether recruiters and freelance agencies are an audience to serve.

## Product Purpose

Personal site and blog of Jan-Philip Loos, Freelance Platform & Product Engineer in Hamburg. It
exists to win engagements: success is a technical decision-maker booking a call (the author's
calendar booking link). The blog and gems demonstrate depth and earn trust and inbound interest.

## Positioning

- **Product Engineering, end to end:** the guiding principle. One person who covers the whole path,
  from infrastructure and platform to UI, and from modern product practice (small increments, fast
  feedback from real users) to engineering and team processes (trunk-based development, continuous
  delivery, engineers who own outcomes instead of tickets).
- **Hands-on, and the knowledge stays:** builds alongside the team rather than advising from the
  side, so the team owns and sustains what was built after the engagement ends.
- **Functional programming + DevOps:** a rare combination, part of the claim.
- **Proven at scale, founder's perspective:** from startup co-founder to systems at 100M+ requests a
  day.
- **Not:** cheap offshore capacity, a pure advisor, or an architect who doesn't code.
- Industries (e-commerce, healthcare, B2B SaaS, fintech so far) describe past and current work, not
  a binding focus.

## Operating Context

- Visitors evaluate a person, not a product: they scan the home page, check proof and past work,
  read a post or two, then book a call or come back later.
- Home page in German on maxdaten.de (`/`) and English on www.maxdaten.io (`/en`); blog, gems, posts
  and author pages are English only.
- Content (posts, gems, authors, tags, series) lives in Sanity; the site is prerendered, so new
  content appears after a redeploy.

## Product Principles

1. Proof over promises: every claim traces to the evidence above.
2. End to end is the offer: show the whole path from infrastructure to product and team, not a
   single specialism.
3. Self-sufficiency is the outcome: the client's team owning the result is the point, not ongoing
   dependency.
4. Hands-on credibility: the site itself, its writing and its engineering demonstrate the craft.
